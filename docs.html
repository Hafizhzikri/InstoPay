InstoPay / Introduction

Introduction

Welcome to InstoPay — onchain payroll management built on Arc blockchain.

InstoPay is an onchain payroll management system built on Arc blockchain. It lets any company manage employees and distribute salaries directly in USDC — no banks, no intermediaries, instant transfer.

⚡

Instant

USDC lands in employee wallets in seconds, not days.

🔒

Transparent

Every payroll run is permanently recorded on blockchain.

🌐

Multi-Network

Supports Arc Testnet and Arc Mainnet. Switch anytime.

🛡️

Non-Custodial

InstoPay never holds your USDC. Funds stay in your wallet.

💡 InstoPay is multi-tenant — every wallet that connects becomes its own independent company. Your employee data is isolated from other users.

How It Works

1

Register Employees

Add employees with their name, position, NIK, wallet address, and net USDC salary. Stored permanently on-chain.

2

Record Attendance

Log monthly attendance: Present, Leave (Cuti), Excused (Izin), or Absent. Used to calculate proportional salary.

3

Approve USDC

Allow the InstoPay contract to transfer USDC from your wallet. One approval per payroll run.

4

Run Payroll

One transaction sends USDC directly to all selected employee wallets. History recorded on-chain forever.

Quick Start

From zero to first payroll in 5 minutes.

ℹ️ You need a Web3 wallet (MetaMask recommended) and testnet USDC. Use the Get test USDC button in Arc Studio sidebar to get free testnet USDC.

Step 1 — Connect Wallet

Click Connect Wallet in the top-right corner of InstoPay. Your wallet address becomes your company identity on-chain. All employees and payroll history are linked to this address.

Step 2 — Add Your First Employee

Go to Employees → click Add Employee. Fill in:

Step 3 — Run Your First Payroll

  2. Click Run Payroll — sends USDC to all selected employees
✅ After the transaction confirms, USDC will appear in each employee's wallet immediately. Check History to see the on-chain record.

Managing Employees

Register, update, and manage your employee roster on-chain.

Employee Data Structure

Each employee record stored on-chain contains:

FieldTypeDescription
walletaddressEmployee's wallet address — USDC is sent here
salaryNetuint256Net monthly salary in USDC (6 decimals)
namestringEncoded as "Name||Position||NIK"
activeboolWhether employee receives salary in next payroll
💡 The name field encodes three values: "Full Name||Position||NIK". This allows storing rich employee data in a single on-chain string field.

Adding an Employee

1

Open Employees page

Navigate to Employees from the sidebar.

2

Click Add Employee

Fill the form: Name, Position, NIK, Wallet Address, Net Salary.

3

Confirm on MetaMask

Transaction calls registerEmployee() on the smart contract.

Editing an Employee

Tap the edit icon on an employee card. Update any field and confirm. Calls updateEmployee() on-chain.

Removing an Employee

Tap the trash icon and confirm. Calls removeEmployee() — uses swap-and-pop for gas efficiency.

⚠️ Removing an employee is irreversible. Their history in payroll runs is still recorded in the on-chain PayrollRun records, but their employee record is deleted.

Active vs Inactive

Toggle the active flag to include/exclude an employee from the next payroll without deleting their record. Inactive employees are skipped during runPayrollSelected().

Attendance System

Record monthly attendance to calculate proportional salaries.

ℹ️ Attendance is optional. If no attendance data is recorded for an employee, they receive their full net salary (safe default).

Attendance Status Codes

CodeStatusCounts for Salary?
0Not yet recordedYes (full salary default)
1Hadir (Present)Yes
2Cuti (Paid Leave)Yes
3Izin (Excused)Yes
4Tidak Masuk (Absent)No

Proportional Salary Formula

When attendance is recorded, salary is calculated as:

effectiveSalary = (Hadir + Cuti + Izin) / workingDays × salaryNet

Default working days: 26 days/month (configurable per company via setWorkingDays()).

Example

EmployeePresentLeaveAbsentNet SalaryEffective Salary
Budi20331,000 USDC(20+3)/26 × 1000 = 884.6 USDC
Sari2600800 USDC26/26 × 800 = 800 USDC

Recording Attendance

Go to Attendance → select the month → set status for each employee per day or bulk-set monthly totals. Confirm the transaction on MetaMask.

Running Payroll

Distribute USDC salaries to selected employees in one transaction.

Before You Start

  • Your wallet must have sufficient USDC balance
  • Employees must be registered and set to active
  • Connect wallet and be on Arc Testnet or Mainnet

Step 1 — Select Pay Period

Use the month-year calendar picker to select the pay period (e.g. October 2026). This label is recorded permanently on-chain as reference for the payroll run. It does not affect the transfer amount.

Step 2 — Select Employees

Check/uncheck individual employees to include in this payroll run. The total USDC required updates in real-time as you select/deselect. Maximum 50 employees per batch.

💡 You can run payroll for a subset of employees — useful when paying different teams at different times.

Step 3 — Approve USDC

Click Approve USDC. This calls USDC.approve(contractAddress, totalAmount) — it authorizes the InstoPay contract to transfer exactly that amount from your wallet.

⚠️ Approval must be done before every payroll run, or when adding more employees. The allowance equals the exact total of selected employees' salaries.

Step 4 — Execute Payroll

Click Run Payroll. The contract calls runPayrollSelected(period, wallets[]) which transfers USDC directly to each selected employee's wallet.

What happens on-chain:

  1. Contract receives the payroll call from your wallet
  2. For each selected wallet: safeTransferFrom(you → employee, salary)
  3. If a transfer fails (e.g. blocklisted address), it emits SalaryFailed and continues — other employees are not affected
  4. A PayrollRun record is stored with runId, period, total, count, timestamp
  5. PayrollRun event emitted on-chain

Payroll History

Every payroll run is permanently recorded on the blockchain.

PayrollRun Record Structure

FieldTypeDescription
runIduint256Auto-incrementing run ID per company
periodstringPay period label, e.g. "October 2026"
totalAmountuint256Total USDC transferred (successful transfers only)
employeeCountuint256Number of employees successfully paid
timestampuint256Unix timestamp of the payroll execution
statusenumAlways Completed
ℹ️ History is wallet-scoped. You only see payroll runs from your connected wallet. Other companies' data is not visible.

Viewing History

Navigate to History in the sidebar. Each card shows the period, date, total USDC, employee count, and run number. Click ArcScan to view the transaction on the blockchain explorer.

FAQ

Common questions about InstoPay.

Can multiple people use InstoPay?

Yes. InstoPay is fully multi-tenant. Every wallet address is an independent company. Your employee data is completely isolated from other users — they cannot see or modify your payroll data.

What happens if a salary transfer fails?

If one employee's transfer fails (e.g. the address is blocklisted), InstoPay emits a SalaryFailed event and continues paying the other employees. The payroll run still succeeds — only that one employee is skipped.

Does InstoPay charge any fees?

No. InstoPay never takes a fee or percentage from your payroll. 100% of the approved USDC goes directly to employee wallets. You only pay the Arc network gas fee (paid in USDC, very small).

Can I recover an employee I deleted?

No. removeEmployee() is irreversible on-chain. Their historical payroll runs are still recorded, but their employee record is deleted. Re-add them as a new employee if needed.

Is employee data private?

Employee names, wallet addresses, and salaries are stored on a public blockchain. Anyone with the contract address can technically read the raw storage data. Do not store sensitive personal information beyond what is needed.

How do I move from Testnet to Mainnet?

  1. Test thoroughly on Arc Testnet with testnet USDC
  2. Go to Settings → Switch to Mainnet
  3. Re-register your employees (Mainnet has a separate contract)
  4. Ensure you have real USDC on Arc Mainnet
  5. Run payroll — transactions are real and irreversible
