01 · The Problem

Payroll is Broken

Every month, millions of companies run payroll through banks with 2–5 day settlement delays, intermediaries that charge 1–3% fees, and opaque systems with no verifiable audit trail.

"Why is paying someone still a 3-day process in 2026? Banks haven't solved this in 50 years."
⏳

2–5 Day Settlement

Employees wait days after payday to actually receive their money.

💸

1–3% Fees

For a $1M payroll, that's $10,000–$30,000 in fees per month — just to move money.

🌍

Cross-border Hell

International wire transfers cost $25–50 each and take 3–7 business days.

🔒

No Audit Trail

Payroll records live in private databases. No employee can independently verify their payment history.

02 · The Solution

InstoPay — Payroll on the Blockchain

InstoPay lets any company register employees and pay their salaries directly to employee wallets in a single blockchain transaction. No banks. No intermediaries. No delays.

04 · Architecture

Multi-Tenant Smart Contract

The PayrollOS contract on Arc is fully multi-tenant: any wallet that connects becomes its own isolated company. There is no global admin. The contract is a public utility.

// Core payroll flow
registerEmployee(wallet, salaryNet, name, active)
  → stored in mapping[msg.sender][wallet]

runPayrollSelected(period, wallets[])
  → USDC.transferFrom(employer, employee.wallet, salary)
  → emits SalaryPaid(runId, wallet, amount) per employee
  → stores PayrollRunData permanently on-chain

Security Properties

🛡️

ReentrancyGuard

All fund-moving functions protected against reentrancy attacks.

🔄

Per-transfer try/catch

One failed payment never blocks others. Partial batches always succeed.

⚡

Batch Cap (50)

Max 50 employees per transaction — protects against gas limit issues.

🏦

Zero Custody

Contract never holds USDC. Funds flow directly employer → employee.
