Q&A Prep

Anticipated Questions

"Why Arc instead of Ethereum or Base?"
Arc is the only chain where USDC is the native gas token. For payroll, this means employers pay fees in the same currency as salaries — no need to hold ETH. Plus sub-second finality and stable predictable fees.
"How does the employer load USDC?"
Currently via any wallet. V2 roadmap includes Circle Onramp Kit so companies load USDC directly with a bank transfer or card.
"What about companies without crypto wallets?"
Circle Developer-Controlled Wallets solves this in V2 — employers log in with email, Circle manages wallet custody transparently.
"Is this audited?"
Built-in security review caught and fixed two critical vulnerabilities before deployment. Not a formal third-party audit yet — that's on the roadmap for mainnet production use.
"What's the business model?"
Three options: (1) small protocol fee per payroll run, (2) premium features on subscription, (3) white-label licensing to HR software companies. Open to Circle's input.
"How does it handle taxes?"
Net salary only currently — employer calculates net pay after taxes. A tax module is on the V2 roadmap for specific jurisdictions.
